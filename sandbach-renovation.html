Sandbach Renovation

Sandbach · Full two-storey refurbishment & extension

Client Project · Sandbach

A full renovation and extension, inside and out.

Sandbach was a comprehensive project for our client — a two-storey home taken back to brick internally and extended, then rebuilt and finished from top to bottom. We managed every trade, from groundworks and structural openings to the final coat of paint.

New bathrooms, reconfigured living spaces, a rebuilt extension and a refreshed façade — all delivered end to end on a single contract, with one point of contact from concept to completion.
