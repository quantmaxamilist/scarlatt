Naseby Road

Congleton · Two-bedroom bungalow — full renovation

Personal Development · Congleton

A tired two-bed bungalow, completely reimagined.

Naseby Road came to us dated and dark, with a closed-off layout that never made the most of its footprint. We took it on end to end — stripping it back and rebuilding it into a bright, open home finished to the standard we put our name to.

The heart of the project is a reconfigured open-plan kitchen and living space that draws light through new windows and flows out to a fully landscaped garden. Every room was renewed: a new fitted kitchen, a contemporary bathroom, fresh flooring and decoration throughout, and new kerb appeal with off-road parking.
