Alsager Kitchen

Alsager · Open-plan kitchen & living renovation

Client Project · Alsager

An open-plan kitchen, reconfigured and rebuilt.

For this Alsager home our clients wanted a brighter, more open heart to the house — a kitchen and living space that worked for everyday family life, rather than the closed-off, dated layout they'd inherited.

We stripped the existing arrangement right back, formed new structural openings and rebuilt the space around a bespoke kitchen — managing every trade from groundworks and structure through to first and second fix. A vaulted skylight was added to pull daylight deep into the plan.

The result is a seamless open-plan kitchen, dining and living space: a hand-finished kitchen with a central island, new flooring and fresh decoration throughout, and a bright, connected home delivered from concept to completion.
