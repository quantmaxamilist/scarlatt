Alsager Kitchen

Alsager · Open-plan kitchen & living renovation

Client Project · Alsager

An open-plan kitchen, reconfigured and rebuilt.

For this Alsager home our client wanted a brighter, more open heart to the house. We stripped the existing layout back, formed new structural openings and rebuilt the space around a bespoke kitchen — managing every trade through to the finish.

A vaulted skylight, new flooring, fresh decoration and a hand-finished kitchen and living space — a complete transformation delivered from concept to completion.
